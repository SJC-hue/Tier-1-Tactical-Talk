My Story

From there to here

Each stage of my life has cost me something. Here's the short version of how I got from there to here.

I was a father at 17 and a national bodybuilding champion at 20. At 21, my four-year-old daughter was killed by a drunk driver.

I left England two weeks later. I didn't know how else to survive it.

I spent the next twelve years in government and contract service, much of it overseas in demanding, high-pressure environments. I came home with two gunshot wounds, two helicopter crashes behind me, and PTSD I spent years outrunning.

What followed was a series of complete reinventions: beach lifeguard, four Ironman triathlons, a career in corporate sales, and a move to Spain. Then came classical ballet — at an age when most men are winding down — alongside turning my home into a hospice for terminally ill rescue cats.

In recent years I've come through two heart attacks and cancer. I'm clear now, and I'm still training.
