Know how to keep yourself safe.

Practical personal safety training for men and women, taught by a former U.S. Special Forces operator. Workshops for groups, corporate businesses and workplaces. Private sessions are also available for individuals.

Most safety advice is a list of things to be afraid of. I teach a way of thinking: notice early, plan ahead, and act with confidence. What other people choose to do is never your fault. My job is to give you more options.

Your instructor

Simon Cobrettie

Founder, Tier 1 Personal Safety

I served with the U.S. Department of Defense as an operations and strategy specialist in Special Forces. Military and Intelligence for 12 years.

That work was about reading a situation early, planning for what could go wrong and staying calm when it does. These skills are not secret or reserved for soldiers. I teach the everyday version: how to walk home, travel alone, go out at night, meet people online and spot trouble before it reaches you.

How we can work together

Every session is built around your routine, your city and the things you actually worry about.

Group workshops

A relaxed, practical session. Mixed or women-only if you prefer. Basic class: 1 to 1.5 hours, 10 to 14 people.

One-to-one safety plan

We go through your commute, home, social life and online habits, then build a plan that fits your life. In person or by video, single session or ongoing, fully confidential.

Travel briefings

Heading away for work, alone or with friends? I look at transport, accommodation, nightlife and what to do if plans go wrong. Destination-specific, for solo and small-group trips, quick turnaround available.

Assessments and specialty projects

For people and organizations that want a specific problem looked at and solved, not just a class.

  • Risk mitigationSpot the realistic risks in your life, work or business and agree simple steps to reduce them.
  • Threat reductionCut down what makes you a target, from what you share online to how you handle a tense encounter.
  • Pattern of lifeReview your own routines, such as your commute, gym times or school run, to find what is predictable and change what matters.
  • Home safetyA walk-through of your home with low-cost fixes for doors, windows, lighting and daily habits.
  • Bug-out bagsBuild a ready-to-go bag and a plan for leaving fast in a fire, flood, blackout or other emergency.
  • Regional travel preparednessA briefing for the country or region you are visiting: local risks, transport, emergency numbers and what to carry.
  • Physical and digital securityPractical upgrades to locks and habits, plus phones, accounts, privacy settings and location sharing.

What you'll learn

Clear, practical skills you can use the next day. No jargon and no scare tactics.

  • Reading situationsNotice what is normal around you, so you spot what is not and have time to react.
  • Trusting your instinctsHow to act on an uneasy feeling early, without feeling rude or paranoid.
  • Boundaries and de-escalationVoice, words and body language that end an unwelcome approach and lower the temperature.

Questions people ask

Do I need to be fit or have any experience?

No. Most of what I teach is awareness, planning and communication. Anyone of any age or fitness level can take part.

Do you teach fighting?

The focus is on avoiding trouble and getting away early, because that is the best outcome. However, we do cover "Last Resort".

Will this make me anxious?

That is the opposite of the goal. Sessions are practical and calm. You leave with a plan and more confidence, not a list of fears.

Can I organize a group of friends or a women-only session?

Yes. Groups are my favourite format, and women-only sessions are available on request.

Is any of the material classified?

No. I teach general principles for everyday life, not operations. I do not discuss specific missions or units.

I am being followed, stalked or threatened right now. Can you help?

Yes, but please contact the authorities first. In Spain, call 112 in an emergency and 016 for the gender violence helpline. I can help you plan once you have specialist support in place, but I am not a replacement for the police or a victim support service.

Where and when do you teach?

Barcelona and the surrounding area, online worldwide, and elsewhere by arrangement. Daytime, evening and weekend sessions are available.
