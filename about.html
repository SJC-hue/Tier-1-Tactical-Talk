My Story

From There to Here

Each stage of my life has cost me something. Here's the short version of how I got from there to here.

I was a father at 17 and a national bodybuilding champion at 20. At 21, my four-year-old daughter was killed by a drunk driver. I left England two weeks later and spent the next decade as an Operations Specialist and doing overseas contract work. I came home with two gunshot wounds, two helicopter crashes behind me, and PTSD I spent years outrunning.

What followed was a series of complete reinventions: beach lifeguard, four Ironman triathlons, corporate sales, and a move to Spain. Then came classical ballet—at an age when most men are winding down—alongside turning my home into a hospice for terminally ill rescue cats. In recent years, I've come through two heart attacks and cancer, and I'm now clear.
